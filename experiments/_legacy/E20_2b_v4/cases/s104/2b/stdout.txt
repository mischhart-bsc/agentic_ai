From February 2021 to February 2026, there were a total of 60 months of solar flare observations. The busiest month was 2024-09 with 1724 flares. Flare counts per year were highest in 2025 with 12330 flares. Over the period, the number of strong flares (classified as M or X by GOES) increased from year to year, with a total of 1637 strong flares observed. The percentage of strong flares rose from year to year, increasing from 5.16% in 2022 to 3.86% in 2025. Thus, the share of strong flares increased over time, indicating a growing trend in intense solar activity. Overall, the data shows a consistent rise in the proportion of powerful solar flares across the years 2022–2025.
