The monthly number of solar flares evolved over the period from February 2021 to February 2026, with a clear pattern of variation in flare frequency. The share of strong flares (GOES mean flux ≥ 6e-7) did not increase consistently from 2022 to 2025; instead, the data shows a fluctuating trend. Due to discrepancies in the data, the reported trend and magnitude of strong flares cannot be trusted as accurate. The analysis confirms the need for robust data validation and accurate field definitions before drawing conclusions. The data covers a five-year period and shows no major disruptions or anomalies in the flare pattern, though the magnitude of strong events remains uncertain.
