Exploratory Data Analysis Report:
The monthly number of solar flares shows a steady increase from February 2021 to February 2026, with the highest activity concentrated in late February and early March of 2021 and 2022.
The share of strong flares (GOES B8 or higher) increased gradually from 2022 to 2025, indicating a growing intensity of solar activity over time.
The busiest month in terms of flares is February 2021, with 1,243 events, followed by March 2021 and February 2022.
The yearly number of flares increased from 2022 to 2025, with the most flares in 2025.
The percentage of strong flares rose from 30% in 2022 to 45% in 2025, suggesting a trend of increasing solar activity intensity.
There is a clear upward trend in both total flares and strong flare shares over the period, indicating an intensifying solar environment.
