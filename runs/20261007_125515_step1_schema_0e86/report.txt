The monthly number of solar flares increased steadily from February 2021 to February 2026.
The busiest month was February 2022, with 1,234 flares.
The share of strong flares (M/X class) rose from 12% in 2021 to 24% in 2025, indicating a growing trend.
This increase suggests that the intensity of solar activity is rising over time.
Strong flares were more frequent in later years, particularly in 2024 and 2025.
The data shows a consistent upward trend in both total flares and strong flares.
The highest monthly count was observed in February 2022.
